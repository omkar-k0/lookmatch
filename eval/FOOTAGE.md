# Footage credits

All eval clips are from Wikimedia Commons under public domain, CC0 or CC BY licences.
The clips are not stored in this repo; `make footage` downloads them and checks each sha256.

Frames per clip come from `make frames` (`eval/sample_report.json`). Clips with 0 frames were too dark for the sampler and are not used.
